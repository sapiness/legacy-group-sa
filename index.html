LEAKLESS VALVE


Meet the Patented Leakless Valve – a key component of our international water solution. From mould to finished product, all under one roof. No leaks, no compromise


Our Leakless Valve is a patented water-saving device for toilets. Designed by South African innovator Paseka Lesolang. The valve's primary purpose is to tackle the immense problem of water waste from toilets, which are often the largest water consumers in a home. It prevents up to 70% of water loss caused by toilet leaks
