SERVICES

PRODUCTS & SERVICES


At Legacy Ventures Group SA, we offer a comprehensive and integrated portfolio of plastic manufacturing solutions designed to meet the diverse and evolving demands of our clients across multiple industries.

Custom Injection Moulding

We deliver tailored injection moulding solutions, ranging from intricate prototype development to full-scale, high-volume production runs. Our capabilities support a wide variety of applications, including automotive components, consumer goods, packaging solutions, and industrial parts. Each product is manufactured with precision, consistency, and strict adherence to client specifications.

Product Development & Design Support

Our experienced team provides end-to-end support throughout the product lifecycle from initial concept and design through to prototyping and final production. We offer detailed mould engineering, design optimisation, and feasibility studies to ensure products are not only functional and innovative but also cost-effective and ready for efficient manufacturing.

High-Volume Production

Leveraging advanced injection moulding technology and a skilled workforce, we are equipped to handle large-scale production requirements. Our processes are designed to ensure efficiency, repeatability, and consistent quality, enabling us to meet tight deadlines and high-demand supply needs without compromising on standards.

Quality Assurance & Sustainability Consulting

Quality is embedded in every stage of our operations. We implement rigorous quality control measures to ensure all products meet industry standards and client expectations. In addition, we are committed to sustainable manufacturing practices, focusing on reducing material waste, improving energy efficiency, and supporting environmentally responsible production processes.

Strategic Sourcing & Supply Solutions

Through our established industry partnerships and extensive supply network, we are able to source and deliver specialised plastic products that may fall outside standard production requirements. This ensures our clients benefit from a broader product offering and a reliable, end-to-end supply solution.

Leak-less Valve

As part of our commitment to innovation and sustainability, we offer the Leak-less Valve™, an advanced water-saving solution designed to detect leaks and prevent unnecessary water loss in toilet cisterns. Legacy Ventures Group SA holds exclusive rights to manufacturing this innovative product, reinforcing our position as a leader in practical, high-impact engineering solutions that deliver measurable value across both commercial and public sector environments.
