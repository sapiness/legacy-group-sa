ABOUT

Who We Are

Legacy Ventures Group SA is a 100% Black-owned plastic manufacturing company based in Marlboro, Sandton, specialising in injection moulding, precision plastic products, and in-house toolmaking. We are not traders or intermediaries, we are a fully operational manufacturer with advanced production facilities, a dedicated toolroom, and a highly skilled team of machine setters, operators, and toolmaking professionals.

In addition to our core manufacturing capabilities, the CEO of LVG SA is the innovator behind the Leak-less Valve, a water-saving solution designed to detect leaks and prevent water loss in toilet cisterns. This product reflects our commitment to innovation, sustainability, and practical engineering solutions for everyday challenges.
