OUR INHOUSE TOOLROOM

We believe in keeping it real. Our toolroom is undergoing upgrades right now, but it hasn’t slowed down our in-house mould manufacturing.

From lightweight water bottles to UV-resistant fish oil containers, and from deodorant roll-ons to chemical-grade lubricant jugs – we cover the spectrum. All manufactured in-house.

We are a leading plastic manufacturing company specialising in injection moulding and precision-engineered products.
